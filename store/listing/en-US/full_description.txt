Jahongirlar is a 2D strategy battle game starring the great commanders of history.

Lead Genghis Khan, Jalal al-Din Mingburnu, Amir Timur, Bayezid the Thunderbolt, Babur and Alp Arslan: gather an army, mine gold with your workers and bring down the enemy fortress.

⚔️ 6 COMMANDERS — 6 CAMPAIGNS
• Every commander has a historical map of regions, rivers and mountains
• 1500 levels, with a powerful boss at the end of each region
• Unique hero powers: Steppe Storm, Indus Leap, Strength in Justice, Thunderbolt Charge, Cannon Barrage, Seljuk Raid

🏰 STRATEGY
• Workers, swordsmen, spearmen, archers and cavalry — each strong against another
• Give attack, defend and retreat orders, or take direct control of a soldier
• Tactics: caravan, shield wall, war drum and more

⭐ PROGRESSION
• Earn stars to unlock new campaigns
• Upgrade your army in the Workshop
• Chests, daily rewards and 20 achievements
• Outfits: choose your army's colors

📜 HISTORY
• A short historical fact after every victory
• No blood or gore — fun for the whole family

🌍 Languages: Uzbek, English, Russian, Turkish
📶 Play offline
3 save slots and 3 difficulty levels: Normal, Hard, Legend.
