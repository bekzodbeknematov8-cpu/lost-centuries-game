# Jahongirlar — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-15_

This policy explains what data the game **Jahongirlar** ("the app") handles. The app is made by
an independent developer ("we"). Contact: **[CONTACT_EMAIL]**

## 1. Data the app stores on your device
- Game progress (levels, stars, coins, rubies, upgrades, outfits, achievements) and settings
  (language, volume) are saved **only on your device** (`progress.json`, `settings.json`).
- We do not have servers and we do not receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## 2. We do not collect personal data
The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos or any
account. There is no chat and no user-generated content.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app can show **optional rewarded ads** (for example, "watch an ad to double your coins").
Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:
- the device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
- approximate (IP-based) location, device and app information,
- ad interaction data (views, clicks) and diagnostics.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. See
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[how Google uses information from apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
In regions where it is required (EEA, UK, Switzerland) the app asks for your consent before
showing personalized ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings
(Settings → Google → Ads).

## 4. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)
Optional purchases (ruby packs, starter pack, "no ads") are processed by **Google Play**.
We do not see or store your payment details. We only receive a confirmation that the purchase
was completed, which is used to deliver the item.

## 5. Children
The game has no blood or graphic violence and is suitable for a general audience, but it is not
specifically directed at children under 13. If the app is offered to children, ads are requested
in a child-safe (non-personalized) mode as required by Google Play's Families policy.

## 6. Security
Progress files stay on your device. Network connections (ads, purchases) are made by Google
libraries using encrypted connections (HTTPS).

## 7. Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address and the
"Last updated" date will change.
